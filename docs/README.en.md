# RE3: Behind the Shoulder 🧟

**Language:** English | [Português (Brasil)](README.pt-BR.md)

> An independent 3D reconstruction experiment inspired by *Resident Evil 3: Nemesis* (1999), featuring an over-the-shoulder camera and enemy AI research.

**Current status:** Planning. No playable build is available yet.

## 💡 The idea

What would it feel like to explore classic RE3 environments with a modern over-the-shoulder camera while preserving the atmosphere and appearance of the 1999 game?

This project investigates the feasibility of building 3D environments faithful to the original visual identity and experimenting with updated controls, aiming, and enemy behavior. It is neither a completed remake nor a promise to perfectly reproduce the entire game.

## 🎯 Goals

- Study the structure and file formats of the classic PC releases.
- Rebuild one small test area in 3D with faithful proportions and visual style.
- Implement an over-the-shoulder camera, movement, and aiming.
- Experiment with more engaging enemy AI without losing the game's identity.
- Investigate ways to reproduce interactions, puzzles, and scripted events.
- Document successes, failures, decisions, and lessons learned on GitHub.

Recreating the full campaign is a **long-term vision** dependent on technical feasibility. Our first tangible milestone is a working room.
